# Microscope — imaging session

> **EXAMPLE.** Placeholder text that shows the format. It is not a validated procedure: replace it with your own.

**Where:** [[MIC-01]] on [[TBL-01]] · **Camera software:** [[LAP-02]] · **Slides and cover slips:** [[CAB-01.S2]] ·
**Lens paper:** [[PED-01.D3]]

## Before you start

- [ ] Book the microscope.
- [ ] Read COSHH-014 if you'll use immersion oil or cleaning solvents (linked on the microscope's page).
- [ ] Objectives clean; lowest magnification in place.

## Procedure

1. Switch on the lamp, then start the camera software on [[LAP-02]].
2. Place the sample, focus at low magnification, then move up.
3. Use immersion oil only with the oil objective; wipe it off with lens paper when done.

## Shutdown

1. Lowest objective back in place, lamp off, dust cover on.
2. Save and copy your images before logging off.

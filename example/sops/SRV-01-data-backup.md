## Purpose

Make sure every instrument PC's data exists in at least two places.

## Procedure

1. On each PC, check the last automatic copy to [[SRV-01]] ran and finished.
2. Copy anything that failed manually.
3. Once a month, copy the server's data to a USB drive from [[PED-03.D1]] and store it away from the lab.
4. Note the date and your initials in the backup log.

# Freezer alarm — what to do

> **EXAMPLE.** Placeholder text that shows the format. It is not a validated procedure: replace it with your own.

**Applies to:** [[FRZ-01]] (−80) and [[FRZ-02]] (−20) · **FRZ-01's socket:** [[OUT-03]], its own circuit

## Purpose

What to do when a freezer alarms, so samples aren't lost while someone decides what's wrong.

## Safety

- PPE: cryo gloves and eye protection for the −80.
- Don't hold the door open for long: every minute open makes things worse.

## Procedure

1. Check the door is fully closed and the seal isn't iced up.
2. Read the display temperature and note it with the time.
3. Check the freezer has power: plug seated, and [[OUT-03]] live.
4. If the temperature keeps rising, move the most valuable samples to the other freezer.
5. Call facilities and record what happened in the log.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Alarm, temperature normal | Door ajar or sensor fault | Close the door; watch for 15 minutes |
| Display dead | No power | Check the socket; don't move it to a shared socket |
| Temperature rising steadily | Compressor fault | Move samples, call facilities |

# UV-ozone cleaner — substrate cleaning

> **EXAMPLE.** Placeholder text that shows the format. It is not a validated procedure: replace it with your own.

**Where:** [[UVO-01]] on [[BENCH-04.A]] · **Spare lamp:** [[CAB-01.S2]] · **Spare fuses:** [[BENCH-02.D1]]

## Purpose

Remove organic residue from ITO and glass substrates, and make them wettable, just before spin coating.

## Safety

- PPE: lab coat, gloves, UV-blocking safety glasses.
- Hazards: UV light, ozone, hot lamp housing.
- Stop immediately if: you smell ozone outside the unit, or the lid opens while the lamp is on.

## Before you start

- [ ] Substrates already cleaned in the ultrasonic bath ([[BATH-01]]) and dried with nitrogen.
- [ ] Substrate tray clean; substrates face up, not touching.
- [ ] Room above the unit: its lid needs 40 cm to open fully (keep the shelf above clear).

## Procedure

1. Lift the lid and load the tray.
2. Close the lid; the interlock must click.
3. Set the time (typically 15 min) and start.
4. When it finishes, wait 5 minutes for the ozone to break down before opening.

## Shutdown and clean-up

1. Take the substrates straight to the spin coater or the glovebox: the clean surface lasts minutes, not hours.
2. Wipe the tray with IPA and leave the lid closed.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Won't start | Lid not latched | Close it firmly and check the interlock |
| Films dewet after cleaning | Lamp ageing | Log it; the spare lamp is in [[CAB-01.S2]] |
| No display at all | Blown fuse | Unplug and report it; spares are in [[BENCH-02.D1]] for whoever is authorised to replace them |

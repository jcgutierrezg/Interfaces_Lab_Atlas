# Benchtop centrifuge — routine operation

> **EXAMPLE.** Placeholder text that shows the format. It is not a validated procedure: replace it with your own.

**Where:** [[CEN-01]] on [[BENCH-04.A]] · **Tube adaptors:** [[CAB-01.S2]] · **Spare fuses:** [[BENCH-02.D1]]

## Purpose

Routine spins of sealed tubes, up to the rotor's rated speed.

## Safety

- PPE: lab coat, gloves, eye protection.
- Hazards: rotor imbalance, aerosols from cracked tubes, the lid mechanism.
- Stop immediately if: loud vibration, a burning smell, or the lid unlocks while spinning.

## Before you start

- [ ] Rotor and buckets clean, dry, and undamaged.
- [ ] Right adaptors for your tubes ([[CAB-01.S2]]).
- [ ] Tubes filled to equal mass; balance an odd number with a blank.

## Procedure

1. Load tubes opposite each other so the rotor is balanced.
2. Close the lid until it latches.
3. Set speed and time, then start.
4. Wait for the rotor to stop completely before opening.

## Shutdown and clean-up

1. Remove the tubes and wipe any spill with the approved disinfectant.
2. Leave the lid open so condensation can dry.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Won't start | Lid not latched | Close it firmly and check the latch |
| Shakes or walks | Unbalanced load | Stop; rebalance by mass, not volume |
| No display at all | Blown fuse | Unplug and report it; spares are in [[BENCH-02.D1]] for whoever is authorised to replace them |
